New quick launch bar under the home grid, for apps you rarely open but want fast (camera, notes). Long-press an app and drag it in; drag along the bar to reorder, out onto the grid to remove. Bar apps leave the grid, still count in the stats and stay in search. With sync, the bar is the same on all phones. Empty, the bar says to drag apps in; settings can turn it off. The grid has one row fewer while the bar is on.
