TurboLaunch is a small, search-first home screen for Android, made for GrapheneOS and other Google-free phones.

Press Home, type two or three letters, hit Enter, and the app opens.

* Every installed app in one list over your wallpaper, work profile apps included
* A search box always docked at the bottom, within thumb reach
* Long-press an app for its app info
* No tracking, no ads, no Google Play Services

Permissions and features you should know about:

* Accessibility service (optional, off by default): TurboLaunch actions only switches the screen into split screen to open an app pair. It reads no screen content and sees nothing you type.

TurboLaunch is in early development. Coming next: fuzzy search, a home grid filled by how often you launch apps, wallpapers, and optional sync of launch counts to your own S3 bucket.
