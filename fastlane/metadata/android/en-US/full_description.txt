TurboLaunch is a small, search-first home screen for Android, made for GrapheneOS and other Google-free phones.

Press Home, type two or three letters, hit Enter, and the app opens.

* Fuzzy search like fzf: "orgm" finds Organic Maps, app shortcuts included
* A home grid that fills itself with your most-launched apps and never moves an icon once placed
* The search box always at the bottom, within thumb reach
* Long-press an app to remove it from home, hide it, see its app info or uninstall it
* Clock, date and battery; long-press to hide the grid while sharing your screen
* Double-tap empty space to lock, swipe down for notifications
* App pairs: two apps side by side in split screen, one tap away
* A stats screen showing launch counts and where each app sits
* Home and lock screen wallpapers, grid size and font sizes
* Work profile apps included
* Export and import of settings, pairs and the home grid as one file
* No tracking, no ads, no Google Play Services

Permissions and features you should know about:

* Accessibility service (optional, off by default): TurboLaunch actions only locks the screen on a double-tap, pulls down notifications and switches into split screen for an app pair. It reads no screen content and sees nothing you type.
* Expand status bar: a swipe down on the home screen opens the notification shade. Without the accessibility service this uses a hidden Android call that home apps have long relied on; if a future Android removes it, the swipe simply does nothing.

TurboLaunch is in early development. Coming next: optional sync of launch counts and home cells to your own S3 bucket.
